# ASO Keyword Research — per market

How users actually search the budgeting category on Google Play, by locale. NOT translations of "budget app" — the real head term differs per country.

## How Google Play ranking works (read first)

- Google Play has **no keyword field** (unlike the App Store). It indexes ranking text from, in order of weight:
  1. **App title** (≤30 chars) — strongest signal. One head keyword here is worth more than ten in the description.
  2. **Short description** (≤80) — second strongest. Front-load the head term.
  3. **Full description** (≤4000) — repeat each head term **3–5×** naturally (headers + body). Do not keyword-stuff.
- So "picking keywords" = deciding which terms go in the **localized title + short description**, then weaving them through the full description.
- Each locale is indexed separately, so a localized title with the local head term is the single biggest ASO win.
- Validate exact volumes/difficulty in an ASO tool (AppTweak / Sensor Tower / Google Keyword Planner) before finalizing — the lists below are category knowledge, prioritized by typical demand.

## English (en)

- **Head (high volume, hard):** budget app · expense tracker · money manager · budget planner · spending tracker
- **Mid (category):** personal finance · track expenses · family budget · finance app · money tracker · bill tracker
- **Long-tail (easier, high intent):** AI budget app · scan receipt expense · shared budget app · bank import expenses · subscription tracker · budget app for couples
- **Note:** "expense tracker" and "budget app" are the two giants — both belong in title + short.

## Russian (ru)

- **Head:** учёт расходов · контроль расходов · семейный бюджет · личные финансы
- **Mid:** трекер расходов · домашняя бухгалтерия · планирование бюджета · управление деньгами · накопления
- **Long-tail:** сканирование чеков · общий бюджет · импорт из банка · учёт подписок · бюджет для семьи
- **Note:** "учёт расходов" + "домашняя бухгалтерия" are the classic heads. RU demand on Play comes largely from RU-speakers in EU/PL, not RU itself.

## Polish (pl) — home market, prioritize

- **Head:** budżet domowy · wydatki · kontrola wydatków · finanse osobiste
- **Mid:** aplikacja do wydatków · menedżer wydatków · oszczędzanie · planowanie budżetu · zarządzanie pieniędzmi
- **Long-tail:** skanowanie paragonów · wspólny budżet · import z banku · śledzenie subskrypcji · budżet dla par
- **Note:** "budżet domowy" (household budget) is THE dominant head — must be in title/short. "kontrola wydatków" = strong purchase intent.

## Spanish (es)

## Placement checklist (per locale)

1. Set the **localized title** from the table above.
2. Make sure the **short description** opens with the head term (e.g. DE short should literally contain "Haushaltsbuch", NL "huishoudboekje", PL "budżet domowy").
3. In the **full description**, ensure the head term appears in the opening line AND in at least one section header, 3–5× total.
4. Keep one secondary head term (e.g. "expense tracker" / "kontrola wydatków") in the first paragraph.
5. Re-check after any copy edit that the head term survived.
