Monefy vs AI Budget Assistant: Which One Fits You?

Author: Mikhail Peraviortkin · 2026-10-07 · Updated 2026-06-19

Monefy and AI Budget Assistant are both expense trackers, but they are built for different habits. Monefy is a small, fast app for logging spending by hand. AI Budget Assistant adds an AI chat, voice input, receipt scanning, shared accounts and bank-statement import. This page compares them side by side so you can pick without installing both. It is based on each app’s public pages as of October 2026, and apps change, so check the current listing before you decide.

Which app is faster for plain manual expense logging?

Monefy. Its own site describes recording a purchase in two taps and puts simplicity, speed and privacy first. If all you want is to tap an amount, pick a category and move on, it is hard to beat. AI Budget Assistant has a manual form too, but it is a larger app built around more than quick logging.

Which app has AI features?

AI Budget Assistant. You can type or say “spent 45 on groceries” in the AI chat, and the app asks you to confirm before it saves anything. You can also scan a receipt, including line items, and ask questions about your spending. Monefy’s site lists no AI features. The free plan includes 50 AI requests per month; higher limits and some advanced AI features need a paid plan.

Which platforms does each app support?

Monefy has apps for Android and iOS. AI Budget Assistant has an Android app and a web app at ai-budget.pl that you can add to your home screen like an app. There is no native iOS app. If you are on an iPhone and want a store app, Monefy fits better; if you also want a browser on a laptop, AI Budget Assistant does.

Which one costs less?

Both start free. Monefy’s core app is free, with an optional Premium upgrade that, according to its help center, includes unlimited accounts. AI Budget Assistant’s free plan gives you 3 accounts and 50 AI requests a month; Pro is $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial. Monefy’s Premium price depends on your store and country, so read its listing for the current figure.

Which is better for a shared family budget?

AI Budget Assistant. It has shared accounts where each member is an Owner, Editor or Viewer, so a partner can add expenses and a child can only look. Adding more members needs Pro, which allows up to 5 per account. Monefy’s sync via Google Drive or Dropbox keeps your own data on your own devices; it is not a multi-person budget.

Can I take my Monefy data with me?

Yes. AI Budget Assistant has a dedicated Monefy import that keeps the categories you built. Transfers between your own Monefy accounts and opening balances are skipped, because they are bookkeeping rather than spending. The full walkthrough is in switching from Monefy, Wallet or Money Manager, so it is not repeated here.

When Monefy is the better choice

  • You use an iPhone and want a native app from the App Store.
  • You want the simplest possible tracker with no AI and no extras.
  • You prefer your data to stay on your device or in your own Google Drive or Dropbox.
  • You need an interface in one of its 25+ languages and yours is not among our nine.

When AI Budget Assistant is the better choice

  • You want to log expenses by chat or voice, or scan receipts.
  • You share a budget with a partner or family and want roles.
  • You import bank statements from mBank, PKO, Revolut or Wise and want them categorized.
  • You want a web app next to your Android phone.
  • You want CSV, PDF or Excel reports without paying.

How to move from Monefy

Export from Monefy, upload the file in the import section, check the preview, and confirm. Duplicate detection means re-uploading an overlapping file is safe. Details are in the switching guide. You can try it free at ai-budget.pl or on Google Play. If you are also weighing price, see what a free budgeting app really means, or compare with Wallet by BudgetBakers.

FAQ: Monefy vs AI Budget Assistant

Is Monefy free?

Monefy’s core app is free, with an optional Premium upgrade. According to its help center, Premium includes unlimited accounts. The exact Premium price varies by store and country, so check its listing.

Does AI Budget Assistant work on iPhone?

There is no native iOS app. You can use the web app in Safari and add it to your home screen, which gives it its own icon. It needs an internet connection.

Is AI Budget Assistant free?

Yes, there is a free plan with 3 accounts and 50 AI requests per month. Pro costs $4.99 per month or $29.99 per year and includes a 7-day free trial.

Can I import my Monefy history into AI Budget Assistant?

Yes. Export your data from Monefy and upload it in the import section; categories carry over. Transfers between your own Monefy accounts and opening balances are not imported.

Which is better for a family budget?

AI Budget Assistant, because shared accounts support Owner, Editor and Viewer roles. Monefy syncs your own data between your devices rather than sharing one budget between people.
