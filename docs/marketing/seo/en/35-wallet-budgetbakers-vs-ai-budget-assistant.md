# Wallet by BudgetBakers vs AI Budget Assistant: Which One Fits You?

Wallet by BudgetBakers and AI Budget Assistant solve the same problem in different ways. Wallet leans on connecting your bank accounts and has a very wide language and platform reach. AI Budget Assistant leans on an AI chat, voice input, receipt scanning and statement import, with no live bank link. Below is a side-by-side look so you can choose. It is based on each company's public pages as of October 2026; prices and plans change, so confirm on the current listing.

## At a glance: Wallet vs AI Budget Assistant

| | Wallet by BudgetBakers | AI Budget Assistant |
|---|---|---|
| Platforms | Web, iOS and Android | Android app and a web app (installable to the home screen); no native iOS app |
| Price | Free to start; paid Premium with a free trial | Free plan; Pro $4.99/month or $29.99/year, 7-day trial |
| Bank connection | Automatic bank connection | No live bank link; you import statements (CSV/PDF) |
| AI | Learns categories from synced bank transactions | AI chat, voice input, receipt scanning with line items |
| Shared / family | Positions itself for personal and family finances | Shared accounts with Owner, Editor and Viewer roles (members need Pro) |
| Import | Bank sync | Polish banks, Revolut, Wise, plus Monefy, Wallet and Money Manager exports |
| Reports and export | Data export is a paid Wallet feature | CSV, PDF and Excel reports, free on every plan |
| Languages | 50 | 9 (English, Polish, German, Spanish, French, Russian, Ukrainian, Belarusian, Dutch) |
| Security statements | ISO 27001 certified and GDPR compliant, per its site | Optional end-to-end encryption you switch on in Settings |

## Which app connects directly to my bank?

Wallet. Its site advertises automatic bank connection and categorization that learns from synced transactions, which saves manual work. AI Budget Assistant has no live bank link. You download a statement from your bank (mBank, PKO, Revolut, Wise and others) and upload it; you see a preview before anything is saved.

## Which app has AI features?

AI Budget Assistant is built around them. You can type or say an expense into the AI chat and confirm it, scan a receipt with line items, and ask questions about your spending. The free plan includes 50 AI requests per month, and advanced AI features need Pro. Wallet's strength is bank-driven automation; see its site for its own assistant products.

## Which platforms does each app support?

Wallet runs on the web, iOS and Android. AI Budget Assistant has an Android app and a web app at [ai-budget.pl](https://ai-budget.pl) that you can add to your home screen, but no native iOS app. If you want a store app on an iPhone, Wallet is the option of the two. If Android and a browser cover you, either works.

## Which one costs less?

Both start free. Wallet offers a paid Premium plan with a free trial; its price depends on region and billing period, so read the current listing. AI Budget Assistant's free plan gives 3 accounts and 50 AI requests a month; Pro is $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, with a 7-day trial. Compare what each free tier lets you do, not only the headline price.

## Which is better for sharing a budget with family?

Both target households. AI Budget Assistant offers shared accounts with Owner, Editor and Viewer roles, so a partner can add spending while a child can only view. More than one member needs Pro, which allows up to 5 per account. Check Wallet's own plan page for how it handles sharing before you commit.

## Which app supports my language?

Wallet lists 50 languages; AI Budget Assistant offers 9: English, Polish, German, Spanish, French, Russian, Ukrainian, Belarusian and Dutch. If your language is one of our nine, the interface, the chat and the in-app help are available in it. If not, Wallet is the safer bet.

## When Wallet is the better choice

- You want your bank transactions pulled in automatically.
- You use an iPhone and want a native app.
- Your language is not one of our nine.
- You want a long-established product with certifications it states on its site.

## When AI Budget Assistant is the better choice

- You prefer entering spending by chat, voice or receipt scan over linking a bank.
- You want to control what gets in by importing statements and reviewing a preview.
- You want shared accounts with roles, plus a web app next to your Android phone.
- You want CSV, PDF or Excel reports on the free plan.

## How to move from Wallet

Wallet's own data export is a paid Wallet feature, so export before you cancel Premium. Then upload the file in the import section, check the preview and confirm. AI Budget Assistant drops Wallet's transfer rows, since they are not spending. Try it free at [ai-budget.pl](https://ai-budget.pl) or on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.budget.assistant). Weighing a simpler tracker? ## FAQ: Wallet by BudgetBakers vs AI Budget Assistant

**Does AI Budget Assistant connect to my bank automatically?**

No. There is no live bank connection. You import a statement file from your bank, review the preview, and confirm. Supported sources include mBank, PKO, Revolut and Wise.

**Is Wallet by BudgetBakers free?**

You can start free, and Wallet also has a paid Premium plan with a free trial. Prices vary by region, so check its current listing.

**Can I import my Wallet data into AI Budget Assistant?**

Yes. Export from Wallet and upload the file in the import section; your categories carry over. Wallet's transfer rows are not imported because they are not spending. Wallet's export is a paid Wallet feature.

**Does AI Budget Assistant have an iPhone app?**

Not a native one. You can use the web app in Safari and add it to your home screen. It needs an internet connection.

**How much does AI Budget Assistant cost?**

There is a free plan with 3 accounts and 50 AI requests per month. Pro is $4.99 per month or $29.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial.
