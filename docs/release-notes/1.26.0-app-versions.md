# Release notes for admin -> App Versions - 1.26.0

Store / in-app `UpdatePrompt` release notes for version **1.26.0**, one block per
locale. Locales: `en`, `ru`, `ua`, `pl`, `es`, `fr`, `de`, `be`, `nl`.

**A minor, not a patch.** Renaming, deleting and pinning a chat conversation is
a new capability, not a fix, so it is 1.26.0 rather than 1.25.1 — the same
reasoning as the previous bump.

**Most of this release lives in the browser and stays there.** Six screens —
transactions, analytics, budgets, the dashboard, settings, chat — got a real
desktop layout at window widths of 1024px and up. None of it changes anything
on the phone, which is why the store text gives it one line.

**The pin is described as personal, not shared.** It reorders your own list
only, and you can pin a conversation someone else shared with you without
moving it for them. Saying "pin a conversation" without that would read as a
change to the conversation itself, not to your own view of it.

**Deleting a shared conversation is stated as irreversible and account-wide.**
It disappears for every member, not just whoever deleted it, and there is no
undo — the note says so rather than letting "delete" read as personal-only.

**Nothing here promises a benefit to someone who has not updated.** Renaming,
deleting and pinning are new client actions with nothing to see on a phone
still running the previous build, regardless of what changed on the server.

## en

```
New in 1.26.0:
- Chat conversations can now be renamed, deleted, or pinned to the top of your list — long-press one in your history, or tap its new "⋯" menu. A pin only changes your own view, and a pinned conversation gets a pin icon. When a conversation can be shared, its sharing pill now shows a small arrow so you can switch it. Deleting a shared conversation removes it for everyone, with no undo.
- Fixed: switching accounts or signing out could leave a previous account's — or a previous person's — numbers on screen, in your "Safe to Spend" figure, your "Inflation Shield" prices, and your chat history. All three now clear correctly.
- Fixed: recording your voice for an expense or income and leaving the screen mid-recording could leave the microphone on. It's released the moment you leave.
- Fixed: a link from an app directory could quietly fill in the wrong referral code when you registered. It no longer touches it.
- And if you open the app in a browser, it now has a proper desktop layout.
```
