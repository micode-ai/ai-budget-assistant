# Brand voice — AI Budżet (for community posts)

Read by `upstream/METHOD.md` in place of its own `references/brand-voice.md`. METHOD.md's rule
holds: the **community's** tone wins over this one when they conflict (say so when it happens);
the banned-words list below is a floor that always applies.

## Project
- Name: **AI Budżet** (Polish), **AI Budget Assistant** (English). Site `ai-budget.pl`, web app
  `app.ai-budget.pl`, Android on Google Play. No iOS app.
- What it is: a personal and family budget app where AI does the data entry — scan a receipt,
  say or type an expense, ask questions about your money in plain language.
- Made by: a solo maker (MiCode). Free tier; paid Pro/Business.
- Feature facts: `docs/wiki/features/`, never memory.

## Target audience
People in Poland managing a personal or household budget (couples and families sharing one),
and English-speaking budgeters for the English communities.

## Voice & tone
A maker talking to peers: plain, specific, a bit self-deprecating, curious about feedback.
Leads with a real problem or a concrete thing the app does, not with the product name. Polish
copy uses "ty" forms (as the app does), not "Państwo".

## Banned words & phrases
- EN: game-changer, revolutionary, seamless, unlock, supercharge, effortless, all-in-one
  (as a boast), "in today's fast-paced world", "take control of your finances", "the best app"
- PL: rewolucyjny, przełomowy, bezproblemowo, "przejmij kontrolę nad finansami", "najlepsza
  aplikacja", "zmieni twoje życie", "w dzisiejszych czasach"
- Any comparison naming a competitor app negatively.
- "saves you X zł" / "zaoszczędzisz X zł" or any figure the wiki does not document.

## Formatting constraints
- No emoji unless the community's own posts use them.
- No em dashes in post copy (METHOD.md rule).
- Links: one, at the end, and only where the rules allow; otherwise "link w komentarzu" or none.

## Do / Don't
- Don't: "AI Budżet to rewolucyjna aplikacja, która zmieni Twoje finanse!"
- Do: "Robię aplikację do budżetu domowego i utknąłem na jednym: jak zachęcić drugą osobę w
  związku, żeby też wpisywała wydatki? Jak to u was działa?"
